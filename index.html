How this model works

Uses completed matches in the same competition and provider’s current season, with real halftime scores. Combines recent first-half goals scored/conceded, home/away splits, and a Poisson goals model with observed total-goal frequencies. Older results receive less weight.

Qualification requires at least 8 recent results per team, 4 at the relevant venue, 30 league results, agreement between both teams’ records, and a lean that remains strong when expected goals change by 20%. Status is rechecked before a result qualifies. Picks expire at scheduled kickoff.

Scores are uncalibrated model leans, not proven win probabilities. Lineups, injuries, xG and opponent-strength adjustments are not included. No automatic bets. An Under 2.5 lean will often be higher because it covers more scorelines; that alone does not establish a better bet.

The free feed has delayed scores and schedules. Confirm kickoff independently before acting. Season restrictions may prevent analysis. The bot reports API errors and does not replace unavailable current fixtures with old matches. History is cached in memory for 30 minutes; refreshing clears it.
